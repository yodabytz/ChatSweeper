Free:
Five successful delete/archive actions combined, lifetime allowance, not per batch.
Counts, title search, manual selection, pin protection, review screens, progress,
Stop, and themes remain available. Export-before-delete is available for the trial.

Pro:
$1.99/month. Proposed optional annual plan: $19.99/year (confirm before setup).
All features and no ChatSweeper-imposed cleanup quota; ChatGPT limits still apply.
Advanced age filters, automatic older-history loading and standalone bulk exports
are proposed Pro features.

Required before paid publication:
1. Choose the billing provider and establish the publisher's billing account.
2. Build hosted checkout and a self-service cancellation/subscription portal.
3. Deploy an authenticated entitlement/usage service with verified billing webhooks.
4. Implement trial quota, paid entitlements and feature gating in the extension.
5. Reserve quota before a batch, count only confirmed successful operations,
   release unused reservations, and handle retries/concurrent tabs consistently.
6. Test successful payments, failed payments, refunds, cancellation, expiry,
   offline/license-server failures, reinstall/account recovery, and quota exhaustion.
7. Describe billing data, retention, processors and contact information in the policy.
8. Update the store listing/screenshots and supply reviewer access instructions.

Do not store payment-provider secrets in the extension, send ChatGPT tokens to
the license server, or transmit conversation content for billing. Client-only
limits are bypassable; server-side entitlements reduce casual abuse but cannot
make an extension's client-side functionality impossible to modify.

The Chrome Web Store's built-in payment system is retired. Use an external
payment provider. Do not present this unrestricted 0.3.1 draft as an enforced
five-chat trial or a functioning subscription product.
