What ChatSweeper does

ChatSweeper helps you count, select, filter, export, archive and delete conversations on ChatGPT. It runs on chatgpt.com and chat.openai.com.

Information processed

The extension reads conversation titles, identifiers, dates, pinned/archive status and your workspace identifier. It uses your existing ChatGPT session and access token in memory to authenticate requests to ChatGPT. Session responses may include account identifiers. When you request an export, it reads the selected conversations' message trees, which may contain personal communications and other information you placed in your chats. It does not read message contents merely to count or filter titles.

Where information goes

Authenticated requests go to ChatGPT on the current ChatGPT origin. The current extension has no developer-operated data-collection server, analytics, advertising, or billing service. It does not sell user data or send chat contents or authentication tokens to the developer. OpenAI's handling of its service is governed by its own policies.

Local processing and exports

Conversation metadata, selections and settings are processed in the current page's memory. Refreshing or closing the page clears this page-session state. Requested exports are downloaded as JSON to your browser's configured download location; they remain there until you remove them and may be handled by your own backup or synchronization software. Attachments are referenced rather than downloaded. The extension does not include your session token in the export file.

Your choices

Deletion and archive batches require review. Deletion is permanent. Archived chats can be restored through ChatGPT. You may stop a batch after the current operation, turn off the extension, or uninstall it. Uninstalling does not restore deleted chats or remove files you downloaded.

Data use

Information is used only to provide the conversation-management features described here. It is not used for advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or purposes unrelated to those features.
