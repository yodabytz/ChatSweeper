NAME
ChatSweeper

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Organize ChatGPT chats: count, filter, export, bulk archive, and delete selected conversations with a review before changes.

SUGGESTED CATEGORY
Productivity / Tools — select the closest option available in the dashboard.

LANGUAGE
English

DETAILED DESCRIPTION
Clean up your ChatGPT sidebar with ChatSweeper.

Click the ChatSweeper button to add checkboxes beside your conversations.
Choose the chats you want to organize, review the list, and archive or delete
them in a batch.

Features:
• Count chats in your current workspace, including archived conversations.
• Search all titles, filter by age, and sort by creation or update date in the organizer.
• Preview recent messages and restore archived chats.
• Undo the last archive batch during the current organizer session.
• Protect pinned chats from selection by default.
• Load older sidebar conversations with one button.
• Review selected titles before archiving or permanently deleting chats.
• Export selected conversations to a JSON file.
• Optionally export before deletion, with a second confirmation after download.
• Follow batch progress, stop the queue, and see failures by chat title.
• Match ChatGPT's appearance or choose light or dark mode.

How to use:
1. Open ChatGPT and reload the page after installing ChatSweeper.
2. Open the sidebar and click the extension icon or the ChatSweeper page button.
3. Wait for chat details to load, select chats, then choose Review archive,
   Review deletion, or Export selected.

Important details:
• Requires a signed-in ChatGPT account and the English ChatGPT interface.
• The compact panel acts on loaded sidebar chats; the organizer covers full history.
  The total count covers the current workspace, not every workspace combined.
• Age filters use the last update date, not the chat creation date.
• Deletion is permanent. Archived chats can be restored in the organizer or ChatGPT settings.
• JSON exports include conversation message trees. Attachments are referenced,
  not downloaded. This is not a full-account export or a ChatGPT import file.
• ChatGPT interface/API changes and rate limits can affect availability.

Privacy:
ChatSweeper communicates with ChatGPT through your existing signed-in session.
The current build has no developer-operated collection server or analytics.
Conversation exports are downloaded to your device when you request them.
See the linked privacy policy for details about local processing and ChatGPT requests.
