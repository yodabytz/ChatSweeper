Upload that ZIP only. Do not upload the outer submission-kit archive,

UPLOAD STEPS
1. Sign in at https://chrome.google.com/webstore/devconsole
2. Register/complete your publisher account if necessary.
3. Choose Add new item and upload the ZIP named above.
4. Use listing.txt and assets/ to fill in the listing.
5. Complete privacy practices using privacy-fields.txt as a starting point.
6. Supply your real publisher name, support email/URL, and public privacy-policy URL.
7. Save as draft while the paid-launch work is completed.
8. Once billing is implemented, upload the final paid build with a higher version,
   update the listing/privacy policy, test the purchase/cancellation flow,
   and submit for review. Store approval is Google's decision.
